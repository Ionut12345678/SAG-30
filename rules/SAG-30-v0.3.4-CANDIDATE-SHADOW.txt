SAG-30 Early Radar v0.3.4 CANDIDATE SHADOW
STATUS: RESEARCH ONLY / NOT BUY / NOT PRODUCTION
PARENT: v0.3.3 FROZEN remains authoritative and unchanged.

PURPOSE
Operationalize previously undefined semantic gates prospectively without changing v0.3.3.
No alert generated by this candidate may be treated as production.

BASELINE / ACTIVITY
- Use 5-minute IEX cumulative-volume baseline at the same ET bucket from prior sessions.
- Candidate-valid baseline requires >=5 prior-session samples and positive median cumulative volume.
- RVOL = current cumulative volume / same-bucket median.
- Activity confirmed at RVOL >=3x.
- Activity strength saturates at 10x for interpretation; values above 10x do not increase confidence linearly.
- <5 baseline samples = insufficient baseline, never candidate-HOT.

LANES
- FRESH: no observed >=+30% session in the previous 5 trading-session dates and not in premarket.
- SECOND_IMPULSE: observed >=+30% in one of the previous 5 session dates. Energy memory alone is routing only.
- OVERNIGHT: observation in 04:00-09:30 ET.
- v0.3.4 candidate-HOT promotion is enabled only for FRESH in this first candidate.
- SECOND_IMPULSE and OVERNIGHT remain tracked but blocked from promotion until their reset/re-wake and hold/continuation semantics are prospectively defined.

PRICE CONVERSION
After activity is confirmed, conversion occurs when a later same-session observation has:
- price > immediately prior valid same-session price;
- change_pct > immediately prior valid same-session change_pct;
- current change_pct <20%.
No minimum positive increment is imposed in this candidate.

ACCEPTANCE
After conversion, acceptance occurs on a later observation when:
- price remains >= conversion price; and
- failed acceptance has not occurred.
Acceptance cannot itself produce candidate-HOT.

FAILED ACCEPTANCE
- price falls below the pre-conversion price (the observation immediately before conversion).
This resets the candidate path for the session.

ABSORPTION / WAIT
After acceptance:
- two consecutive later observations without a new observed high => WAIT/ABSORPTION.
No candidate-HOT promotion while absorbed.

EXPANSION PROOF
Only after acceptance, require at least one:
1. new observed high after acceptance;
2. reacceleration >=2 percentage points from accepted change_pct;
3. two consecutive positive observation intervals with rising observed highs.
Expansion first observed >=+20% => candidate-LATE, zero early credit.

EARLY BANDS
Same reporting bands as v0.3.3:
<5 EXCELLENT; 5-<10 IDEAL; 10-<15 EARLY VALID; 15-<20 SALVAGE; >=20 LATE.

ANTI-HINDSIGHT
- Candidate state is computed only from observations available at evaluation time.
- No backdating.
- Retrieval/source timestamps remain separate.
- Historical replay never earns prospective credit.

VALIDATION

source misses, cadence misses, lane distribution and baseline sample coverage.
Thresholds are frozen for the candidate validation window; changes require a new candidate revision.
