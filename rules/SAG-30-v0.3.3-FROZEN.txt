GLOBAL ARCHITECTURE
DISCOVERY -> CLASSIFY LANE -> ACTIVITY CONFIRMED -> PRICE CONVERSION -> ACCEPTANCE/RECLAIM -> EXPANSION PROOF -> EARLY-HOT

LANE A — FRESH
NEW ACTIVITY -> PRICE CONVERSION -> ACCEPTANCE -> EXPANSION PROOF -> EARLY-HOT.

Discovery can come from:
- new activity appearance
- rank acceleration into Top 20 / Top 10
- verified abnormal RVOL or turnover
- early price response
- catalyst + activity

Activity-only, catalyst-only or rank-only cannot promote EARLY-HOT.

LANE B — SECOND IMPULSE / ENERGY MEMORY
Energy Memory = prior winner >= +30% OR extreme activity/repeated appearance within the previous 5 sessions.
Energy Memory is routing/context ONLY.

Required:
ENERGY MEMORY -> RESET/COOLING -> RE-WAKE -> RECLAIM/ACCEPTANCE -> EXPANSION PROOF -> EARLY-HOT-B

For early credit, Re-Wake should occur < +15%; ideally < +10%.

RESET/COOLING = meaningful retracement/consolidation and/or activity contraction.

FAST RE-WAKE requires:
- prior winner
- reset
- renewed abnormal activity
- price reclaim/reacceleration
- no absorption
- no major source conflict

Prior winner alone is never a signal.

LANE C — OVERNIGHT / PRE-CLOSE
PRE-CLOSE/AH event/activity -> PRICE RESPONSE -> HOLD -> PREMARKET CONTINUATION/EXPANSION.

First qualification +15% to <+20% = SALVAGE.
First qualification >=+20% = LATE and zero early credit.

ACTIVITY
RVOL, turnover and volume acceleration are ONE evidence family.
Raw volume without a valid baseline = interest only.
Routing interpretation:
RVOL 3x-5x = confirmed
5x-10x = strong
>10x = extreme
Above approximately 10x, treat RVOL as saturated; do not increase confidence linearly.

PRICE CONVERSION
Price Conversion means confirmed activity begins producing directional price progress before excessive extension.
Do not invent a fixed numerical threshold beyond the frozen rules. It must be evidenced chronologically from observations.

ACCEPTANCE / RECLAIM
Acceptance means price holds the conversion instead of immediately giving it back.
For Lane B, use credible reclaim after the reset.
Acceptance/Reclaim alone cannot produce EARLY-HOT.

EXPANSION PROOF — DECISIVE GATE
Only after Acceptance/Reclaim, require at least ONE of:
1. a real new high after acceptance;
2. reacceleration of approximately >= 2 percentage points;
3. two consecutive positive observation intervals with rising highs;
4. breakout from a micro-consolidation while activity remains confirmed.

If Expansion Proof is first observed at >= +20%, the structure may be correct but receives zero early credit.

BLOCK / KILL CONDITIONS
ABSORPTION:
Activity/reclaim exists but price makes minimal progress.
Two observations without a new high -> WAIT, not HOT.

FAILED ACCEPTANCE:
Material giveback or loss of reclaim -> do not promote HOT.

LATE:
Confirmation first occurs >= +20% -> zero early credit.

DATA_QUALITY:
