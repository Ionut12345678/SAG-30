SAG-30 MULTI-ENGINE DISCOVERY v0.1 SHADOW

STATUS
Research-only challenger. NOT BUY. Does not modify SAG-30 v0.3.3 FROZEN or v0.3.4r2 semantic gates.

OBJECTIVE
Increase recall of future +30%/+50% winners while they are still below +10%.

ENGINE A - BASE
Existing FAST SCOUT + r2 path remains unchanged.

ENGINE B - HIGH-RECALL SCOUT
Eligible research pool: -10% <= current change < +10%.
A symbol is high-recall eligible when at least one live precursor family is present:
- positive price acceleration,
- positive fresh-turnover impulse,
- turnover in the top 5% of the current sub-10 cross-section,
- or current change > +2%.
Only routing priority is affected. This is not a semantic signal.

ENGINE C - CONTINUOUS RANKER
Score 0-100 from cross-sectional percentiles:
- acceleration 35%
- fresh-turnover impulse 30%
- turnover 20%
- current change 15%.
Weights are hypotheses to be validated prospectively and are not production rules.

DUAL ENGINE
Base shortlist is preserved.
Up to 20 additional challenger symbols may enter deep observation.
A research watchpool retains repeated high-recall candidates; default persistence requires >=2 observations.
No challenger event can emit production outbox alerts.

PRIMARY AUDIT
Winner cohort: symbols that later reach +30%/+50%.
Primary question: was the winner observed, selected, and/or r2-HOT before +10%?
Secondary benchmark: before +20%.

DECISION RULE
Do not tune weights or thresholds from one winner.
Compare base shortlist recall, dual-engine recall, and r2 HOT recall prospectively across a cohort.
Any material semantic change requires a separately versioned challenger.
