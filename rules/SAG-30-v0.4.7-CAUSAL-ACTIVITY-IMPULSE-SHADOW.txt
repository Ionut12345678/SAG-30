Hypothesis
Some FLOW false positives satisfy two positive price intervals on ordinary IEX activity. Requiring the two PRE-ARM-forming bars to show a causal volume impulse versus only PRIOR observed bars may improve FLOW discrimination.

Feature
At base FLOW PRE-ARM:
- V1,V2 = IEX 1m volumes on the exact two consecutive positive event-time intervals that create PRE-ARM.
- BASE = median volume of up to the 20 most recent observed bars strictly BEFORE the first of those two intervals.
- Require at least 5 prior observed bars. Otherwise ACTIVITY_IMPULSE is UNKNOWN.
- ACTIVITY_IMPULSE = mean(V1,V2) / BASE when BASE>0.
No bar at/after PRE-ARM and no session outcome data may enter BASE.

DEV selection

- combined precision >=8%,

Choose eligible threshold with lowest combined FPR; tie-break higher recall, higher precision, lower threshold.
If none eligible: DEV FAIL, do not inspect threshold-specific HOLDOUT results.
