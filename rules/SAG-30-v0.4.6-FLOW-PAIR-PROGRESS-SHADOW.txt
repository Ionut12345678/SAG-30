Evidence before freeze
v0.4.5 corrected baseline on immutable date-matched data:
- EVENT recall/FPR = 29.33% / 17.00%.
- FLOW recall/FPR = 12.00% / 34.33%.
- EVENT OR FLOW recall/FPR = 39.67% / 45.67%.
- FLOW-only winners/nonwinners = 31 / 86.
Thus FLOW false-positive discrimination is the next bottleneck. Do not weaken EVENT.

Single feature-family hypothesis
The existing FLOW rule requires two consecutive positive event-time intervals but imposes no minimum magnitude. Tiny positive drifts can therefore satisfy PRE-ARM. A minimum causal cumulative price progress across the exact two positive intervals that create FLOW PRE-ARM may reject low-energy false positives while retaining enough FLOW-only winners to keep EVENT OR filtered-FLOW recall >=35%.

Feature definition
At the instant base FLOW PRE-ARM first becomes true:
- Let p0 be close_pct at the observation immediately before the first of the two consecutive positive eligible intervals.
- Let p2 be close_pct at the second positive interval / PRE-ARM observation.
- PAIR_PROGRESS_PP = p2 - p0, in percentage points.
Only observations already available at PRE-ARM are used. No future/session outcome data may enter the feature.
If p0 is unavailable, PAIR_PROGRESS_PP is UNKNOWN and the candidate filter does not pass.

DEV selection rule
For each threshold, keep EVENT unchanged and replace FLOW with base FLOW AND PAIR_PROGRESS_PP>=threshold.
Eligible thresholds must satisfy on DEV:
- combined EVENT OR filtered-FLOW recall >=35%
- combined balanced precision >=8%
- median combined signal lead to first +30 >=3 minutes.
Among eligible thresholds choose the one with LOWEST combined nonwinner FPR.
Tie-break in order: higher combined recall, higher combined precision, lower threshold.
If no positive threshold is eligible, this family is DEV FAIL and HOLDOUT is reported only for unchanged baseline; do not invent a threshold.

HOLDOUT PASS requires ALL:
