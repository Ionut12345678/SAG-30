SAG-30 v0.4.1 FAST-PATH CALIBRATION SHADOW
Status: PRE-REGISTERED RESEARCH / NOT BUY
Parent: v0.4 FAST-PATH SHADOW
Production control: v0.3.3 FROZEN unchanged.

Purpose
Address four known validation risks:
1. winner-only selection bias,
2. IEX 1-minute coverage gaps,
3. execution realism,
4. vague promotion criteria.

Required denominator cohort
For historical calibration, compare:
A. Winner sessions: intraday high >= +30%.
B. Near-miss/non-winner sessions: reached >= +5% but never reached +30% that session.
The non-winner cohort should be at least the same size as the winner cohort when available.

Required retrospective replay
Replay 1-minute bars prospectively within each session.
No look-ahead features may be used before each timestamp.
Report separately:
- FLOW recall on winners,
- FLOW false-positive rate on >=+5% non-winners,
- PRE-ARM precision,
- TRIGGER precision,
- median lead time to +30/+50,
- percentage of paths where a trigger is theoretically actionable.

IEX data-quality telemetry
For the live watchpool record:
- minute_bar_present,
- consecutive_1m_bar,
- minute_bar_present_rate,
- consecutive_1m_bar_rate.
Never interpret absent FLOW as a model failure when the required 1-minute data was absent.

Execution telemetry
At PREARM_EVENT, PREARM_FLOW and TRIGGER_SHADOW record latest bid, ask and spread%.
A candidate is marked execution-observable only when ask is available.
Historical replay cannot prove executable fills unless quote data is available; bar-only replay remains calibration-only.

PRE-REGISTERED PROMOTION GATES
These gates are fixed before prospective outcome data and may not be tuned from individual winners.

Minimum evidence:
- >= 40 new prospective winner sessions >= +30%;
- >= 15 distinct U.S. trading sessions;
- >= 200 total PRE-ARM events with mature same-session outcomes.

Data quality:
- >= 80% of watchpool observations must have a usable 1-minute bar;
- >= 65% must have a consecutive 1-minute bar where continuity is required.

Effectiveness:
- winner recall under +10% among data-observable winners >= 35%;
- PRE-ARM precision to same-session +30% >= 8%;
- TRIGGER_SHADOW precision to same-session +30% >= 12%;
- median PRE-ARM lead time to +30% >= 3 minutes.

Execution:
- ask available on >= 80% of PRE-ARM/TRIGGER events;
- report precision separately for spread <= 3% and spread > 3%;
- no BUY-SHADOW promotion if the <=3% spread subset does not preserve the effectiveness gates.

Event vs Flow:
Always report EVENT and FLOW separately:
- count,
- precision,
- recall contribution,
- median lead time,
- overlap.
Do not combine them into one headline metric only.

Promotion rule
All minimum-evidence, data-quality, effectiveness and execution gates must pass prospectively.
Historical replay may calibrate or reject hypotheses but can never by itself authorize BUY-SHADOW.
